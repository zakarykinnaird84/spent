Hello Calculator Privacy Policy

Effective 3 November 2026

Who we are

Hello Calculator is made by StudioStudio (Zakary Kinnaird). This policy describes how the Hello Calculator app handles information.

Summary

Hello Calculator doesn’t collect anything about you. There are no accounts, no ads, no tracking, and no analytics. The app never connects to the internet, and nothing you do in it is sent to StudioStudio or anyone else.

Information stored on your device

So your work is still there next time you open the app, Hello Calculator saves the following on your device only:

  • Your calculations and the cards on your timeline
  • Notes you write on cards
  • Secret words you’ve found and your times-table progress
  • App settings, such as the colour of your secret words book

This information is used solely to run the app. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

Sharing

Hello Calculator can make a picture of a card, a secret word, or your progress. Before anything is shared, a grown-up is asked to answer a quick question. Then the standard iOS share sheet opens, and where it goes — Messages, Mail, Photos, or elsewhere — is entirely your choice. StudioStudio does not receive a copy.

Photos

If you choose Save Image when sharing, iOS asks whether Hello Calculator may add pictures to your photo library. The app can only add pictures. It cannot see or read your photos.

What we do not collect

Hello Calculator does not ask for your name, email address, age, location, or contact details. It uses no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking tools, and it does not sync your data to iCloud or to any service operated by StudioStudio.

Downloads through the App Store, device backups, and other services provided by Apple are handled under Apple’s own policies.

Children’s privacy

Hello Calculator is made with children in mind. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children. There are no ads and no links to other websites, and nothing leaves the app without a grown-up check first.

Your choices

Clearing the timeline in the app’s settings removes your calculations. You can turn off photo access at any time in iOS Settings → Hello Calculator. Deleting the app removes everything it has saved from your device, subject to how iOS handles app removal and backups.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the effective date at the top of this page. Material changes may also be noted in an App Store update.
