How do I find secret words?

There are two ways. Tap Abc and spell a word on the keypad, like an old phone: tap a key more than once to move through its letters. Or work out a sum whose answer reads as a word when you turn the calculator upside down — try 7 × 2, then press =.

Words in the book count towards your total and turn the card orange. Any other word you spell is still saved to your timeline.

Where are the words I’ve found?

Tap Secret words at the bottom of the timeline to open your book. Swipe to turn the pages. Found words are ticked off, and the cover shows how many you have. You can change the colour of the book with the swatch underneath it.

How do I share a card or a word?

Tap the share button on any card, on a word you’ve just found, or under the book. The usual iPhone share sheet opens, so you can send it in Messages, save the picture to Photos, or anywhere else.

How do I clear my calculations?

Open Settings (the gear at the top right of the timeline) and choose Clear timeline. Deleting the app removes everything it has saved.

Is my information private?

Yes. Hello Calculator collects nothing, has no accounts or ads, and never connects to the internet. Everything stays on your device. Read the full privacy policy.
